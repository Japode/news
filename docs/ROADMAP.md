## Non-goals

- **Pages in English** japode.com is the English catalogue; this is the Portuguese home
  of a channel that speaks Portuguese, and an English page waits until a video exists in
  English.
- **The product catalogue** The products live at japode.com; an article mentions one
  only when its video is about it. The Viglet sponsor band and the Japode Ads house
  banners sit outside the article, never inside its text.
- **Publishing from a Claude session** Claude prepares; the Alexandre pushes, changes
  the DNS, the YouTube descriptions and the forum posts.
- **Claims beyond a video's sources** An article says what its video and cited sources
  say, nothing more; one with no video, only what its listed sources say. No post is
  written only to fill the calendar.
- **Comments, newsletter or ads** The conversation stays in the YouTube comments: no
  account, no newsletter, no third-party ads. Chosen on 2026-11-11, two exceptions: a
  correction form (Cognito Forms) and the Japode Ads house banners; neither sets a
  cookie before consent.
